24/7 CCTV Monitoring

Proactive system health monitoring — we know your system is down before you do

Most security camera systems are "set and forgotten." They look like they're working — green lights on, monitors displaying. But the moment a camera goes offline, a hard drive fails, or video is lost... you don't know until you try to review footage and get nothing. That's not security. That's a false sense of security. Our CCTV monitoring service watches the health of your system so you're never caught off guard.

What We Monitor

  • Camera offline alerts — Instant notification when any camera goes down
  • Video loss detection — Alerts when a camera stops recording or loses its feed
  • Hard drive health — Monitoring of storage capacity and drive failure warnings
  • Network connectivity — Alerts when your NVR/DVR loses network connection
  • Recording status — Verification that recording is active and continuous
  • System uptime reports — Regular reports showing your system's performance

Why Monitoring Matters

A security camera that's not recording is just an expensive decoration. The reality is that camera systems fail — hard drives crash, network connections drop, cameras get knocked out of alignment. Without monitoring, you could go weeks or months thinking you're protected when you're not.

With Security Systems & More's monitoring service, issues are detected and reported immediately. In many cases, we can resolve problems remotely before you even notice them. For issues that require a technician, we dispatch quickly to minimize downtime.

How It Works

  1. System assessment — We evaluate your existing system or design a new one with monitoring in mind
  2. Monitoring setup — We configure your NVR/DVR for remote health monitoring
  3. Continuous monitoring — Our system watches your system health 24/7
  4. Instant alerts — You and our team are notified immediately of any issues
  5. Fast response — We troubleshoot remotely or dispatch a technician as needed
  6. Regular reports — You receive system health reports so you always know your coverage status

Can You Monitor My Existing System?

In many cases, yes. We can evaluate your existing camera system — even if it was installed by another company — and determine if it can be integrated into our monitoring service. Contact us for a free assessment and we'll let you know your options.
