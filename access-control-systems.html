Access Control Systems

Keycard, keyfob, keypad, and biometric entry systems for HOAs, businesses, and gated communities

Control who enters your property with professional access control systems from Security Systems & More. We design, install, and manage entry systems for HOAs, gated communities, commercial buildings, and businesses throughout Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, and St. Lucie counties. From simple keypad entry to advanced biometric systems with full audit trails, we have the right solution for your property.

Access Control Solutions We Offer

  • Keycard Systems — Proximity card readers for doors, gates, and parking areas. Easy to issue, deactivate, and manage.
  • Keyfob Systems — Compact fob-based entry for convenient tap-to-enter access at multiple points.
  • Keypad Entry — Pin-code access for doors and gates. Ideal for areas with many users or changing access needs.
  • Biometric Readers — Fingerprint and facial recognition for high-security areas where credentials can't be shared or lost.
  • Gate Access Systems — Automated gate entry with visitor call boxes, remote entry, and scheduled access codes.
  • Mobile Credential Systems — Smartphone-based access control — open doors and gates with your phone.

Who Benefits from Access Control?

  • HOAs & Gated Communities — Manage resident, guest, and vendor access with audit trails and time-limited codes
  • Commercial Buildings — Restrict access to sensitive areas, track who enters and when, integrate with time clocks
  • Retail Stores — Protect stockrooms and offices, manage employee access, eliminate key management headaches
  • Car Dealerships — Secure vehicle inventory areas after hours, track lot access
  • Marinas — Control dock and facility access, issue temporary credentials to seasonal guests

Features & Benefits

  • Full audit trails — Know exactly who entered, where, and when
  • Remote management — Add or revoke access from anywhere via web or mobile app
  • Scheduled access — Set time-based permissions for vendors, cleaners, or temporary staff
  • Integration with cameras — Pair access events with video for complete security coverage
  • Visitor management — Issue temporary codes for guests, deliveries, and contractors
  • Anti-tailgating — Options to prevent unauthorized entry behind authorized users
  • No more rekeying — Lost card or fob? Deactivate it instantly without changing locks
