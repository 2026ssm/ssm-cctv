Security Camera Installation

Professional CCTV and security camera installation for South Florida homes and businesses

Security Systems & More designs and installs professional security camera systems throughout Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, and St. Lucie counties. Whether you need a single camera for your home or a complete surveillance system for your business, our experienced technicians handle everything from system design and equipment selection to installation, configuration, and training.

Camera Types We Install

  • IP Cameras — High-definition network cameras with remote viewing capabilities, up to 4K resolution
  • HD-over-Coax Cameras — Upgrade older analog systems to HD without rewiring
  • Dome Cameras — Discreet indoor and outdoor coverage with vandal-resistant housing
  • Bullet Cameras — Long-range outdoor surveillance with weatherproof housing
  • PTZ Cameras — Pan-tilt-zoom cameras for active monitoring of large areas
  • Turret Cameras — Compact design with no glare from IR reflection
  • LPR Cameras — License plate recognition for parking lots and gated entrances

Complete Installation Process

We don't just mount cameras and leave. Our installation process is thorough and professional:

  1. Free site survey — We assess your property, identify blind spots, and recommend camera placement
  2. System design — We design a custom system based on your needs, budget, and property layout
  3. Professional installation — Clean, neat wiring and mounting by experienced technicians
  4. NVR/DVR setup — Recording device configuration with proper storage and retention settings
  5. Remote access setup — Mobile app and web browser configuration so you can view from anywhere
  6. Training — We show you how to use your system, review footage, and manage settings
