Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about security cameras, monitoring, and access control in South Florida

Got questions about security camera installation, CCTV monitoring, or access control? We've got answers. Here are the most common questions we hear from homeowners and businesses across Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, and St. Lucie counties. Don't see your question? Give us a call at (561) 693-2624.

What areas does Security Systems & More serve?

We serve all of Palm Beach County, Broward County, Martin County, and St. Lucie County in South Florida. Our office is located in West Palm Beach, and our field technicians cover the entire four-county region. View our full service area →

Do you install security cameras for both homes and businesses?

Yes. We design, install, and monitor security camera systems for Florida homes, businesses, HOAs, and commercial properties. Every system is customized to the property's specific needs.

How much does security camera installation cost?

Pricing depends on the number of cameras, type of system, and property layout. We offer free security surveys and consultations to provide an accurate quote. Contact us at (561) 693-2624 to schedule.

Do you offer 24/7 monitoring?

Yes. Our monitoring service actively watches your system health and alerts you before issues become problems. We know your system is down before you do.

Do you install access control systems for HOAs?

Yes. We install and manage key card, fob, and keypad access control systems for HOAs, gated communities, and commercial buildings throughout Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, and St. Lucie counties.

Do I need a permit for security camera installation in Florida?

Permit requirements vary by city and county in South Florida. We are familiar with local code inspectors and handle permit requirements as part of our installation process.

What happens if my security system stops working?

With our monitoring service, we detect system issues proactively and often fix them before you even notice. For non-monitored systems, we offer repair services and preventative maintenance plans.

Can you monitor a system that another company installed?

In many cases, yes. We can evaluate your existing system and determine if it can be integrated into our monitoring service. Contact us for a free assessment.

What types of security cameras do you install?

We install professional-grade IP cameras, HD-over-coax systems, PTZ cameras, dome cameras, and bullet cameras from trusted manufacturers. We also install NVRs, PoE switches, and all necessary cabling.

How long does a security camera installation take?

Installation time depends on the number of cameras and complexity of the property. A typical residential installation can be completed in one day, while larger commercial projects may take several days. We'll provide a timeline during your free consultation.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. We offer preventative maintenance plans that include scheduled system checks, camera cleaning, firmware updates, and priority repair service. Regular maintenance extends the life of your system and prevents costly downtime.

Can I view my security cameras remotely?

Yes. All of our systems support remote viewing via mobile apps and web browsers, so you can check your cameras from anywhere with an internet connection.
