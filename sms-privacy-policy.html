SMS Privacy Policy – Twilio Text Message Campaign

Effective Date: August 2, 2026

Security Systems & More ("SSM," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This SMS Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your mobile phone information when communicating with our field technicians via text messages through our Twilio-powered SMS campaign.

1. Company Information

  • Company Name: Security Systems & More
  • Address: 6215 Georgia Ave., Suite I, West Palm Beach, FL 33405
  • Phone: (561) 693-2624
  • Email: office@ssmcctv.com
  • Website: https://ssmcctv.com

2. Purpose of SMS Communications

We use text messaging (SMS) to communicate with our field technicians for operational purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Job assignments and dispatch notifications
  • Service appointment details and scheduling updates
  • Work order information and status changes
  • Emergency and urgent service alerts
  • Internal coordination and operational updates

SMS messages are intended solely for SSM field technicians and authorized personnel. Messages are not used for marketing, promotions, or advertising.

3. Information We Collect

To participate in this SMS campaign, we collect the following information:

  • Mobile phone number of the field technician
  • First and last name of the field technician
  • Job title/role within the organization
  • Message content sent and received through the platform
  • Opt-in and opt-out status for SMS communications

We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or health information) through this SMS system.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Send operational text messages to field technicians
  • Coordinate service calls and dispatch activities
  • Provide timely updates regarding job assignments
  • Facilitate internal communication between the office and field staff
  • Maintain records of communications for quality and training purposes

5. Opt-In and Consent

Field technicians provide express written consent to receive SMS messages as part of their employment onboarding or enrollment in the dispatch communication system. By providing their mobile number and opting in, technicians acknowledge that they will receive text messages from SSM related to their work assignments.

6. Opt-Out and Unsubscribe

Participation in the SMS campaign is voluntary. Field technicians may opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any SMS message received from our system
  • Contacting the office directly at (561) 693-2624
  • Emailing office@ssmcctv.com with a request to unsubscribe

Upon opting out, the technician will be removed from the SMS distribution list. Please note that opting out may affect the ability to receive timely job assignments and operational updates. Alternative communication methods will be arranged for technicians who opt out.

7. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on operational needs and the technician's work schedule. Technicians may receive multiple messages per day during active work periods. No fixed message frequency is guaranteed.

8. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We share information only as necessary to operate the SMS system:

  • Twilio Inc. – Our SMS service provider that facilitates message delivery
  • Internal authorized personnel – Office staff and management who manage dispatch and scheduling

Twilio processes messages in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the information collected through the SMS system, including:

  • Restricted access to message logs and contact data
  • Secure storage of technician contact information
  • Encryption in transit via Twilio's platform
  • Regular review of access permissions

Despite these measures, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

10. Data Retention

We retain SMS message logs and technician contact information for as long as the technician is actively employed or engaged with SSM, and for a reasonable period thereafter for record-keeping and compliance purposes. Upon request, we will delete personal information where no longer required, except where retention is required by law.

11. Carrier Disclaimer

SMS messages are delivered through mobile carriers. We are not responsible for carrier delays, message delivery failures, or carrier-related fees. Standard message and data rates may apply depending on the technician's mobile plan.

12. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time
  • Receive a copy of the data we have collected about you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at office@ssmcctv.com or (561) 693-2624.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify affected technicians of any material changes. The most current version will always be available upon request from the office.
