How This Works

This tool attempts to connect to the specified port from your browser. Results indicate whether the port is accepting connections from the outside.

  • Open — Port is reachable and accepting connections
  • Closed/Filtered — Port is not reachable, not forwarded, or blocked by a firewall

Tip: Make sure your NVR/DVR is powered on and the port forwarding rule is active in your router before checking.
